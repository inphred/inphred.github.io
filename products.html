SWIR VCSELs

The world's first manufacturable short-wave infrared (SWIR) vertical-cavity surface-emitting laser (VCSEL):

  • Eye-safe wavelengths (1,300–2,300 nm) enabling consumer-facing AR/VR and LiDAR applications
  • ~3–12× SNR improvement over NIR (850 nm) with ~1.5–4× range extension
  • >30% power conversion efficiency (PCE) at ~mW per-aperture power
  • Telcordia reliability standards passed
  • Custom solutions available: emitter + ASIC integration, module-level design

GaN · 450 – 570 nm

RC-LEDs

A resonant-cavity (RC) LED operating across the visible blue-green spectrum:

  • High spectral purity (<12 nm full-width at half-maximum)
  • Spatial directionality (<40° divergence) and 1.2–1.5x higher radiance for more signal on target
  • Improved accuracy for heart rate, SpO₂, and novel biomarkers across all skin tones
  • Suitable for ultra-short-reach (USR) chiplet and die-to-die optical interconnects
  • GaN nanoporous DBR compatible with top or bottom emission
